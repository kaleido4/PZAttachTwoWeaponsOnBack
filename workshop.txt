title=Second Back Slot - Carry Two Weapons On Your Back
description=Adds a second back slot to the hotbar, so you can carry two weapons on your back, like a shotgun and a crowbar, each on its own hotbar key.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]"Back 2" sits right after "Back" on the hotbar (key 2). Belts, holsters and other slots move up one.
description=[*]It takes everything the vanilla back slot takes: rifles, shotguns, axes, bats, crowbars, swords, shovels, rackets, pans, guitars.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=[b]Required Strength Level[/b] (0 to 10, default 0): the Strength level a character needs for Back 2.
description=[list]
description=[*]0: Back 2 slot unlocked from the start.
description=[*]1-10: min level to unlock Back 2
description=[*]Only the level in the Skills panel counts. Moodles like hunger, starving or nicotine withdrawal do not change it.
description=[*]If Strength drops below the level (the game only lowers it after weeks without training it), Back 2 goes away and its item stays in the inventory. The slot comes back once Strength is trained back up.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Works in SP.
description=Should work in MP, but it was not tested.
description=Can be added to existing saves. Before removing the mod, take the item off Back 2.
description=
description=Does not replace game files.
